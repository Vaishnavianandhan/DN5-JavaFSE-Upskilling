Help & FAQs

Welcome to the Help page for the Local Community Event Portal.

How to Register for an Event

  1. Go to the Events section on the home page.
  2. Fill in your Full Name, Email, and Preferred Date.
  3. Select the Event Type from the dropdown — the entry fee will be shown automatically.
  4. Optionally write a message or feedback in the text area.
  5. Click Register to submit your form.

Finding Nearby Events

Click the "Find Nearby Events" button in the Events section. Your browser will ask for location permission — click Allow to see your coordinates. If you deny permission, the portal will display an appropriate message.

Saving Your Preferences

When you select an event type, it is automatically saved using localStorage so that the next time you visit, your preferred event is pre-selected.

To remove saved preferences, click the "Clear Preferences" button on the registration form.

Watching the Promo Video

The Events section contains a short promo video. Once the video is ready to play, a message "Video ready to play!" will appear above it. Use the built-in controls to play, pause, or adjust volume.
